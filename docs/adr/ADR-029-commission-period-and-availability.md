# ADR-029: Commission periods calculate, close, hold and release a program's commissions before any money moves

## Status

Accepted — Phase 3.7.

## Context

Calculation runs (ADR-018) and hybrid batches (ADR-028) can be requested for any range, and an approved commission could be posted at once. Operating a plan needs more: a program's timeline of periods that never overlap, calculated once from a fixed input, reviewed, then held and released on a schedule before the ledger moves — with late or backdated business activity kept out of what was already calculated, and corrections still possible until money moves.

## Decision

- **A period per range of one program's timeline.** `mlm_commission_periods` (000035): program, plan version, source account, idempotency key, [from, until), `release_at` ≥ `until`, status, and the moments of each step. Created by `CommissionPeriodManager::create()` under the program's lock: the version must be the program's **active** one then — kept afterwards, whatever is activated later — and the account a system account of the program funding every commission component, the check hybrid batches use (`CommissionFunding`), so one currency. A program's periods never overlap (`from < other.until and other.from < until`); adjacent ones and gaps are fine. The same key and facts return the period; other facts are refused (`ConflictingCommissionPeriod`). Nothing creates a period implicitly, and no period is inferred for past runs. Models are read-only through Eloquent.
- **Status: open → calculated → finalized → released.** Forward only, each once; a failed step leaves the period where it was. The facts never change.
- **Calculation reuses the engines.** `CommissionPeriodCalculator::calculate()` takes the version's `commission.strategy` components by position, then id: none is refused; one is calculated by `CalculationEngine` under `period:<period>:<component>`; two or more by `HybridCalculationEngine` under `period:<period>:hybrid`. `mlm_commission_period_runs` (000036) records which run calculated which component, in order — the relational provenance of every commission of the period. Each run is checked to be its component's, over the period's range, in its program and version, from its account; anything else is `CorruptCommissionPeriod`. There is no transaction around the runs: short metadata transactions only, and each engine keeps its own, binary's serializable one included. A failure leaves the period open; calling again resumes under the same keys, finding committed runs again. The period is `calculated` only once every component's run is linked; a calculated, finalized or released period is returned as stored.
- **Calculating closes the input.** A calculated result must not go stale, and components calculate at different moments, so the input closes when calculation **begins**: an exclusive lock of the period row, then `input_closed_at`. `VolumeRecorder` records a new entry — original or reversal — only after reading, under a shared lock of the same row, the period its **own** moment falls in: none, or one open with its input still open. So an entry either commits before the input closes and is calculated, or is refused (`FinalizedCommissionPeriod`) — never recorded uncounted in a calculated range. A failed calculation leaves the period open with its input closed, so a resumed one reads the same input. Replays of stored entries, run replays and adjustments are untouched. A reversal is judged by its own moment: a later reversal of an entry of a closed period is recorded — the correction belongs to the later range, as source-entry strategies' cutoffs already assume. Genealogy writes are append-only at the present moment, so they need no freeze.
- **Lock order.** Program, then period, then commissions by id. Creating periods takes the program's lock (as genealogy writes do); calculation, finalization and release take the period's row, then — for finalization and release — its commissions in id order; recording volume takes only a shared lock of the one period its moment falls in. Posting and corrections lock commissions and never the period, reading its status instead: no cycle.
- **HELD and AVAILABLE.** `CommissionStatus` gains `held` and `available` (000037 adds `held_at`, `available_at`): APPROVED → HELD → AVAILABLE → POSTED, and HELD or AVAILABLE → CANCELLED. `CommissionLifecycle` gains no public step to them: only finalization and release move commissions there. APPROVED → POSTED remains for commissions calculated outside any period; existing commissions keep their status.
- **Finalization.** `CommissionPeriodFinalizer::finalize()`: a calculated period whose commissions are all approved or cancelled, none with an unresolved binary correction — the same set-based reading posting uses (`UnresolvedBinaryCorrections`). A reversal after the period's end does not stop it: it belongs to later corrections. One transaction locks the period, then its commissions by id, checks again, and moves every approved one to HELD with `held_at`, and the period to `finalized` — all or none.
- **Release.** `CommissionPeriodReleaser::release($period, $at)`: a finalized period, at `$at` ≥ `release_at`, never the clock alone. HELD → AVAILABLE with `available_at = $at`, the period `released` with `released_at = $at`, atomically; cancelled ones stay cancelled. Releasing again returns it unchanged.
- **No money moves in either step.** Available means eligible to post. `CommissionPoster` stays the ledger boundary: a period's commission is posted only when it is AVAILABLE and its period RELEASED — both checked, so a status written raw cannot post early (`CommissionPeriodNotReleased`); a run calculated for a period but not yet linked is recognised by its derived key. The hybrid batch guard, the binary correction guard, net entitlement, `posted_amount` and ledger verification all still apply.
- **Corrections before posting.** A HELD or AVAILABLE commission is corrected like an unposted one: a partial binary correction is `recorded` and it keeps its status, a full correction or a source clawback cancels it — no ledger movement. Posting later pays the net amount. POSTED corrections are unchanged (ADR-021, ADR-026).
- **Totals.** `CommissionPeriodTotals::of($period)`: calculated, adjustments, net entitlement (cancelled and reversed left out) and posted amounts, exactly, with counts by status, in two aggregate queries.

## Consequences

- A program's commissions can be calculated, reviewed, held and released on an auditable timeline, with late activity kept out of calculated ranges and every movement of money still explicit.
- New entries dated inside a calculated or closed period are refused: record them at a moment of an open period.
- Paid status, payouts, withdrawals, settlement, tax, fees, thresholds, scheduling and period screens are Phase 3.8 and later.
