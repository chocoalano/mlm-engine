# ADR-019: The first built-in strategies pay fixed awards per eligible business entry, up the sponsor line as it stood

## Status

Accepted — Phase 2.9.

## Context

The commission core (ADR-018) calculates any registered strategy, but the package shipped none. The first ones every plan needs reward sponsors for their line's business: the direct sponsor, and sponsors further up by depth. Their inputs already exist — immutable volume history with explicit reversals (ADR-009), a temporal sponsor genealogy readable as it stood at any moment (ADR-012) — but proportional pay would multiply a six-decimal quantity by a decimal rate, which can need more than six decimal places of money, and the package has no rounding policy to decide what happens then.

## Decision

- **Two built-in strategies, registered like any other.** `direct-sponsor.fixed` and `unilevel.fixed` are registered by the service provider through `CommissionStrategyRegistry::register()`, the path applications use; they cannot be replaced, and applications register their own beside them. The engine does not know them: no strategy-specific branch, table, component driver or idempotency exists.
- **Fixed awards, never proportional.** Each strategy pays a configured, exact amount in the component's currency — strictly positive, at most one ledger posting — per eligible entry. A volume entry's quantity is a business measurement that makes an entry eligible; it is never multiplied into money. There is no rate, percentage or multiplier, and nothing is rounded, truncated, floored or ceiled. Proportional variants wait for an explicit quantity-to-money and rounding design.
- **Exact parameters, one reading.** `direct-sponsor.fixed` takes exactly `volume_type`, `source_type`, `minimum_quantity` and `amount`; `unilevel.fixed` takes `volume_type`, `source_type`, `minimum_quantity` and `levels`, a non-empty list of `{depth, amount}` with integer depths from 1 to 100, each once. Volume and source types follow the volume identifier rules; `minimum_quantity` is an exact, non-negative quantity one volume entry could store; amounts are exact financial amounts — floats refused everywhere. One parameter parser per strategy serves both validation at `markValidated()` and calculation, which re-validates the stored definition before it runs. The stored parameters are read, never rewritten.
- **No rules.** These variants have no qualification: a component with rules is refused at validation rather than having its rules silently ignored. Later variants may compose qualification or rank explicitly.
- **Eligible entries are originals, as of the run's cutoff.** A source entry is an original volume entry of the run's program, of the configured volume and source types, effective in `[from, until)`, with a quantity of at least the minimum — compared exactly — and not reversed by a reversal effective before `until`. Reversal entries never earn. A reversal in the same range suppresses the original; a reversal at `until` or later belongs to a later range and leaves the original eligible for this one, so a historical run is reproducible as of its cutoff.
- **Later reversals claw nothing back — yet.** A reversal in a later range produces no candidate there, negative or otherwise, and does not touch commissions already calculated or posted. Clawback needs an explicit adjustment policy; until then a reversed, already-paid entry is a known gap to handle outside the package.
- **Historical sponsor attribution.** The sponsor line is the one in effect when the entry took effect: `directSponsorAt` and `ancestorsAt` at the entry's `effective_at` — a sponsorship effective in the same second counts. A sponsor assigned later, or ancestors joined above the line later, earn nothing from it. The current tree is never used for attribution, and the placement tree is never read.
- **Direct sponsor.** The direct sponsor at the entry's moment earns the amount; with no sponsor then, nobody does. A member never earns from their own entry.
- **Unilevel: physical depth, no compression.** The sponsor line is read to the deepest configured depth; the sponsor at physical depth *d* earns depth *d*'s amount if *d* is configured, and nothing otherwise. Gaps stay gaps, levels are matched by their explicit depth — never by list position — and a short line pays only the depths it has.
- **One candidate per entry and depth.** No aggregation: each eligible entry, at each rewarded depth, is one candidate keyed `volume-entry:<entry id>:depth:<d>`, earned at the entry's `effective_at`, traced with the strategy, the source entry's id, types, source id, member, quantity and moment, the minimum, the recipient and depth, and the amount — all exact strings or integers, independent of time and of calculation order.
- **Reads only, inside the run's snapshot.** Entries are read a chunk at a time, in id order, by one shared query on the calculation's connection; sponsor reads go through `SponsorGenealogy` on the same connection. The strategies write nothing; commissions reach wallets only through the existing lifecycle and ledger.
- **One evidence-backed index.** On 300,000 entries across three programs, the source-entry query without a dedicated index walked the primary key — 67,774 rows filtered away for the first 500 results on both MySQL and PostgreSQL, and in total the whole table per run, whatever the range. Migration 000019 adds `(program_id, type, source_type, effective_at)`: MySQL then range-scans the 2,111 candidate rows (full iteration 141.8 ms → 48.0 ms), PostgreSQL bitmap-scans them (40.4 ms → 26.8 ms, buffers per chunk 4,928 → 2,026). The reversal lookup already uses the unique `reversal_of_id` index. No other schema changes.

## Consequences

- Plans can pay direct-sponsor and depth awards now, reproducibly and with a per-entry audit trail from each commission back to the business entry and the sponsorship that earned it.
- A calculation costs one sponsor-line read per eligible entry, and its candidates are held in memory until stored (ADR-018); very large ranges may call for batching later.
- Awards cannot yet follow the size of a sale, recipients are not yet qualified or ranked, and a later reversal is not clawed back: each is a later, explicit design.
- The new index costs a little on every volume write, in exchange for runs whose cost follows the range calculated rather than the whole history.
